Piece Movement Rules (Click to Preview)

Pawn (1 Pt)

Moves forward 1 square (or 2 on first move). Captures 1 square diagonally forward.

Knight (3 Pts)

Moves in an "L-shape" (2 by 1). The only piece that can jump over other pieces.

Bishop (3 Pts)

Moves diagonally any distance across squares of its starting color.

Rook (5 Pts)

Moves horizontally or vertically any distance in straight lines.

Queen (9 Pts)

Combines Rook and Bishop powers. Moves straight or diagonally in any direction.

King (Infinite)

Moves 1 square in any direction. Protect the King to avoid checkmate!

Special Rules & Endgames

Check

Your King is under direct attack by an enemy piece. You must immediately resolve the check by escaping, blocking, or capturing the attacker.

Checkmate

The King is in check and has no legal moves to escape. The game ends immediately and the player delivering mate wins.

En Passant

When an opponent moves a pawn 2 squares forward, you may capture it diagonally with your pawn on the next turn as if it moved only 1 square.

Pawn Promotion

Reaching the opposite end of the board allows a pawn to instantly promote into a Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.
